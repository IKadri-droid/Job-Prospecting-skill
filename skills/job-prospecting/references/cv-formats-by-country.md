# CV and letter customs by country

Common practice only. Customs change and vary by sector: when in doubt, check an official or well-established
local source (national employment service, university careers service) and **tell the person what you decided**.
The person always decides what appears on their CV. Anti-discrimination laws take priority over habits: never add
age, photo, marital status, nationality or health because "it is usual" if the person does not want it.

| Country / area | Document names | Length | Photo | Personal details usually shown | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| United States, Canada (EN) | résumé + cover letter | 1 page (2 if long experience) | **no** | name, city, phone, e-mail, LinkedIn/portfolio | no age, birth date, marital status or photo (anti-discrimination); reverse chronological; achievement bullets |
| Canada (Québec, FR) | CV + lettre de présentation | 1–2 pages | no | as above | same norms as Canada, in French |
| United Kingdom, Ireland | CV + cover letter | 2 pages | **no** | name, town, phone, e-mail | no date of birth or photo; short personal statement at the top is common |
| France | CV + lettre de motivation | **1 page** for students and juniors, 2 max | optional (less and less used) | name, town, phone, e-mail; age not required | work-study: say "alternance", the contract (apprentissage / professionnalisation) and the rhythm; the letter follows a formal structure |
| Belgium, Luxembourg | CV + lettre de motivation | 1–2 pages | optional | as France | language of the region matters (FR/NL/DE) |
| Switzerland | CV + lettre de motivation (FR) / Lebenslauf + Motivationsschreiben (DE) | 2 pages | common | name, address, phone, e-mail; nationality and permit often shown | work certificates (certificats de travail / Arbeitszeugnisse) are often attached |
| Germany, Austria | Lebenslauf + Anschreiben | 1–2 pages | common but **not required** (anti-discrimination law) | name, address, phone, e-mail | tabular, reverse chronological; full application file (certificates, references) often expected; sometimes signed and dated |
| Netherlands, Nordic countries | CV + motivation letter | 1–2 pages | optional | name, town, contact | concise, direct tone; personal profile line common |
| Spain, Portugal | CV + carta de presentación / carta de apresentação | 1–2 pages | still common, optional | name, town, contact | Europass widely accepted |
| Italy | CV + lettera di presentazione | 1–2 pages | optional | name, town, contact | many add a GDPR consent line at the bottom ("Autorizzo il trattamento dei dati personali…"); ask the person |
| European Union, any country | **Europass CV** | 2+ pages | optional | as chosen | standard EU format; accepted everywhere in the EU, useful for foreign diplomas |
| Morocco, Algeria, Tunisia | CV + lettre de motivation (FR) or Arabic | 1–2 pages | common | name, town, contact; age sometimes | French often expected for companies; follow the posting's language |
| Gulf countries | CV / résumé + cover letter | 2 pages | common | nationality and visa status often expected | check the offer; never invent a visa status |
| Latin America | CV / hoja de vida + carta | 1–2 pages | common | name, city, contact | "hoja de vida" in Colombia; photo less common in multinational companies |
| Japan | rirekisho (standard form) + shokumu keirekisho (career history) | form | yes (on the form) | as the form requires | standard templates exist; a free-form CV is used by foreign companies |
| India | résumé / CV | 1–2 pages | optional | name, city, contact | concise; avoid religion, caste or family details |

## Diplomas from another country
Give the original title + the closest local equivalent in brackets, only if an official equivalence exists or the
person confirms it ("Bachelor's degree (≈ Licence, 3 years)"). Otherwise give the level in years of study.
Suggest the official recognition service of the country when relevant (e.g. ENIC-NARIC in Europe).

## Contract words to use
Use the country's own terms in the documents and explain them to the person once:
work-study / apprenticeship (alternance, Ausbildung/duales Studium, apprendistato, aprendizaje/FP dual),
internship (stage, Praktikum, tirocinio, prácticas), fixed-term (CDD, befristet), permanent (CDI, unbefristet),
temp agency (intérim, Zeitarbeit).
